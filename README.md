# VideoChain

A decentralized video content platform for streaming and managing video content with creator monetization on Stacks blockchain.

## Features

- Video content registration with metadata and topic classification
- Creator ownership verification and monetization rights
- Viewing permissions and access control system
- Topic-based content discovery and organization
- Decentralized video streaming platform

## Smart Contract Functions

### Public Functions
- `add-video` - Add new video content to platform
- `update-video` - Update existing video details
- `remove-video` - Remove video from platform
- `transfer-video` - Transfer video ownership to new creator

### Read-Only Functions
- `get-video-info` - Get video details and metadata
- `get-viewing-permission` - Check viewing access rights
- `get-total-videos` - Get total videos on platform
- `get-platform-stats` - Get platform statistics

## Usage

Deploy the contract to create a decentralized video platform where creators can upload their content and manage viewing rights while earning revenue directly.
